A simple editing workflow

You don't need complicated effects to make a good video. Start with the basics and build from there.

01

Import

Bring your video clips, photos, and other media into your project.

02

Build

Arrange your clips on the timeline and remove unnecessary footage.

03

Add Audio

Choose music or sound and adjust the timing and volume.

04

Add Text

Use text to communicate important information and support your story.

05

Polish

Use transitions, effects, color, and timing to improve the final edit.

06

Export

Review your finished video and export it at an appropriate quality.
