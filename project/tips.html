WORK SMARTER

Tips for better edits

You don't need complicated effects to make a video look good. Start with the basics.

01

Tell a clear story

Arrange clips in an order that makes sense. Remove footage that doesn't support the message or mood.

Try this: Watch your video without sound. Can you still follow what is happening?

02

Keep transitions simple

Too many flashy transitions can distract viewers. Clean cuts and subtle fades are often enough.

Try this: Use one or two transition styles throughout a short video.

03

Make audio count

Music can establish the mood, but it should not overpower dialogue or important sounds.

Try this: Listen with headphones before exporting your edit.

04

Make text readable

Choose clear fonts, strong contrast, and enough time for viewers to read each line.

Try this: Preview your video on a phone screen before publishing.

05

Keep your colors consistent

Large differences in brightness and color between clips can make an edit feel disconnected.

Try this: Compare neighboring clips and make small adjustments where needed.

06

Check before exporting

Watch the complete video to catch awkward cuts, spelling mistakes, black frames, or audio problems.

Try this: Play the final export from beginning to end before sharing it.

LEARN AS YOU GO

Common beginner mistakes

Adding effects to every clip

Instead: Use effects to emphasize important moments, not to fill every second.

Ignoring audio levels

Instead: Balance music, dialogue, and sound effects so the main audio stays clear.

Exporting without reviewing

Instead: Watch the finished export and check its quality before posting.
