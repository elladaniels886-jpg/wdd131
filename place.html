About Nigeria

Nigeria is located in West Africa and is one of the most culturally diverse countries on the continent. It is home to hundreds of ethnic groups and languages.

The country has a variety of landscapes, including tropical forests, savannas, mountains, rivers, and coastal areas.

Capital: Abuja

Largest City: Lagos

Continent: Africa
